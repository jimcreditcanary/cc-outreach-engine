How it works

Choose

Take a look at our website to choose your product or email us with your requirements. Then either email info@jppmk.co.uk, telephone 01908 315885 or fill in our request a quote form.

Quote

You'll be emailed a quote, showing the total cost, including set up fees and carriage. You will also be advised of our payment terms.

Order

Either accept the quote or email us to confirm you wish to proceed with the order. At this point we will need an purchase order (if applicable), full delivery and invoicing addresses, order deadline date and artwork requirements.

Proof

Prior to production we will email you a final artwork proof. Once approved we will proceed to production.

Delivery

Your order is delivered.
