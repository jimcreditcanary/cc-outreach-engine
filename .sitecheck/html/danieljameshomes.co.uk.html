Welcome to Daniel James Lettings

Finding the right home or letting the right property should be an exciting experience — not a complicated one. At Daniel James Lettings, we pride ourselves on providing a professional, friendly and personal service to landlords and tenants alike.

Whether you’re a landlord looking for reliable support in managing and letting your property, or a tenant searching for a place to call home, our aim is to make the entire process as smooth and straightforward as possible. We combine local knowledge, industry experience and a commitment to excellent customer service to deliver a letting service you can trust.

Helping Landlords Let With Confidence

Your property is a valuable investment, and choosing the right letting agent is an important decision. We work closely with landlords to understand their individual requirements and provide a service tailored to their property and circumstances.

From marketing your property and arranging viewings through to tenant selection, referencing and ongoing management, we can help take the day-to-day responsibilities out of letting. Our approach is designed to give landlords confidence that their property is being looked after professionally, while helping to attract suitable, reliable tenants.

We understand that every landlord is different, which is why we offer a personal service rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Helping Tenants Find Their Next Home

For tenants, searching for a new home can sometimes feel overwhelming. We aim to make the process easier by providing clear information, helpful guidance and a responsive service from the initial enquiry through to moving in.

Whether you’re renting for the first time, moving locally or simply looking for somewhere that better suits your needs, we’re here to help you find a property that feels right for you.

A Personal Approach to Property

At Daniel James Lettings, we believe that good lettings are built on good relationships. We take the time to listen, communicate clearly and provide honest, practical advice throughout the letting process.

Our goal is simple: to provide a dependable service that landlords can feel confident in and tenants can rely on. We’re here to make property letting feel less complicated, more personal and ultimately more successful for everyone involved.

Professional lettings. Personal service. Local expertise.
