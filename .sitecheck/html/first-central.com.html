Insurance worth paying for

We’re a fast-growing UK insurer, built on our own market-leading technology, providing effortless customer experiences and value that goes beyond price.

Our story

We’re a leading UK-based personal lines insurer, providing motor and home insurance products to 1.4 million customers through brands including 1st Central and Picnic. Since launching in 2008, we have focused on delivering great value to customers through high quality, cost effective insurance products backed up by brilliant service. Our success has been enabled by our innovative technology and our exceptional colleagues.

Who we are

We have built a business with agility at its heart

We have invested over £125m in developing our own technology and enriching our own data, rather than choosing ‘off-the-shelf’ systems. Alongside our entrepreneurial culture, this allows us to deliver greater value, faster.  We’ve made thousands of improvements to our business and to the products and services we offer. Over more than a decade these have resulted in a truly differentiated business.

Our people and culture

Our colleagues are the heartbeat of our business; they make delivering our ambitious plans possible.  Although we’ve grown a lot as a business, we  maintain our agile approach while creating a safe and positive environment for colleagues to thrive in. We’re always on the lookout for talented people that can help us on our growth journey. 

We highly value experience across all sectors (not just insurance!) and believe diverse thinking leads to better solutions. We think 1st Central is an incredible place to work and so do all our colleagues, but don’t just take our word for it.
