Club & Institute Union

Founded in 1862 the Club & Institute Union (CIU) is the oldest Club Union in the world
The CIU is a non-political federation of Clubs, registered as a Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act

It is an advisory and defensive organisation, not a Club, but a Union of Clubs.

It is neutral in matters of politics and religion whilst open for membership to any bona-fide club It is supported and governed by such Clubs as are members.

CIU Member Benefits

The CIU offers its Clubs and Members many benefits above other non-CIU Club memberships
MEMBERSHIP
CIU Clubs benefit from being part of a Union of Clubs with good purchasing power
Members benefit from advice and extensive procedural knowledge of running a Club
CLUBS
Members have access to over 1200 Clubs across the UK
(subject to purchasing a CIU member Associate Card)
Clubs have the buying power of a large organistaion
LEARNING
Training and educational courses available to all members
Recreational competitions for Club members arranged annually
ENGAGEMENT
Annual AGM's held in Blackpool - all current paid-up members are welcome to attend and vote on proposed motions.
Trade Show for Suppliers, Members, Clubs and Branches to attend
