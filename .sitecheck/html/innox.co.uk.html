Meet the team

Who is Chemist4U?

Chemist4U is one of the UK’s largest online pharmacies, trusted by millions nationwide.

 

We’re GPhC‑registered, UK‑based, and run by experienced pharmacists.

 

We’re trusted by the NHS to provide services including dispensing and delivering your repeat prescriptions, which you can order through our website and app. We also offer the NHS free contraception service.

 

From prescription treatments to everyday healthcare, we make it affordable, safe, and discreet - delivered straight to your door from the comfort of home.

What services do you provide as an online pharmacy?

We offer private online consultations for prescription weight loss treatments and over 90 other health concerns, NHS prescriptions managed easily through our app, and a free NHS contraception service. You can also access everyday healthcare and over‑the‑counter treatments, along with free expert advice from our UK‑qualified pharmacists. All our services are UK‑based, GPhC‑registered, and designed to make healthcare safe, simple, and convenient.

Is Chemist4U based and registered in the UK?

Yes, Chemist4U is a GPhC‑registered online pharmacy based in the UK. We’re owned and run by experienced UK‑qualified pharmacists, supported by our team of prescribers, pharmacy technicians, and dispensers – all trained and registered here in the UK. All our medicines are UK‑licensed and sourced from trusted suppliers. When you contact us, you’ll speak directly to our friendly UK‑based team. You can trust Chemist4U to provide safe, genuine medicines and expert healthcare every time.

How can I be sure Chemist4U is safe and legitimate?

You can be confident that Chemist4U is safe, legitimate, and fully regulated. We are registered with and regulated by the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC), accredited by LegitScript, members of the DiCE network, and licensed by the NHS to provide prescriptions online. We follow strict professional standards, and our pharmacy is regularly inspected to ensure we deliver the highest quality care. You can view and verify all of our credentials via the links in the footer of our website

How do I contact Chemist4U?

It’s easy to get in touch with Chemist4U. For order‑related queries, health questions, or to speak directly with one of our pharmacists, you can reach our UK‑based customer care team through our online help centre. If live chat is available, you’ll see the chat box pop up in the corner of your screen. You can also call us on 01695 474 433 for order‑related queries.
